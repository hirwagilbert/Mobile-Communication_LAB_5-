--- Prioritised remediation (merged across frameworks) ---

  [HIGH] P3 Background location (outside workouts)
     - Replace the 'bundled_terms' mechanism with a separate, explicit,
       unticked opt-in screen for this one purpose, shown BEFORE
       collection, with withdrawal as easy as giving consent. [G1 G2 L1
       D1]
     - Suspend the feed to AdNet Partners until the user opts in for that
       named recipient; add a 'Do Not Sell or Share My Personal
       Information' link that also honours the Global Privacy Control
       signal. [G4 C2 L3]
     - Turn off targeted advertising and tracking for every account under
       18 (DPDP) and require opt-in before any sale/sharing for ages 13-15
       (CCPA), or raise the minimum age to 18 with an age gate. [C2b D4]
     - Add a 'Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information' link, or
       stop using precise location for anything beyond the service the
       user asked for. [C3]

  [HIGH] P4 Heart rate and health metrics from wristband
