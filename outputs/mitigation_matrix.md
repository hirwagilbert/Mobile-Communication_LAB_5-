# Mobile malware mitigation control matrix

| Category | Case study | OS-level controls | App-store-level controls | MDM / enterprise controls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spyware (Data theft / surveillance) | Pegasus (NSO Group) | Fast security patching; exploit mitigations (ASLR, pointer authentication); BlastDoor sandbox for iMessage parsing; iOS Lockdown Mode for high-risk users; regular reboot clears non-persistent implants | FINDING: no effective control - delivered by exploits, never through an app store, so pre-publication vetting and store removal do not apply | Compliance policy enforcing minimum OS/patch level with conditional access; mobile threat defense (MTD) checking indicators of compromise; supervised-device restriction of iMessage/FaceTime for high-risk staff |
| Banking / overlay trojan (Data theft + financial) | FluBot (a.k.a. Cabassous) | Per-source 'install unknown apps' permission (Android 8+); Restricted Settings block Accessibility for sideloaded apps (Android 13+); SMS permissions limited to the default SMS app role; on-device Play Protect scan | Limited: app was sideloaded, not published. Post-publication style control still applies through Play Protect scanning of sideloaded APKs and Safe Browsing blocking of the download URLs | Policy blocking unknown sources in the work profile; app allow-listing; MTD phishing-link and Accessibility-abuse detection; conditional access to banking/corporate apps only from compliant devices |
| Ransomware (Financial) | DoubleLocker (Android/DoubleLocker.A) | Android 7+ stops device-admin apps resetting an existing PIN; scoped storage (Android 10/11+) limits which files an app can encrypt; Accessibility consent screens and Restricted Settings; sideload gating | Limited: distributed outside the store. Play Protect on-device scanning and Safe Browsing warnings are the only store-side controls | Enforced cloud backup of corporate data; remote lock/wipe and re-provision; block unknown sources; containerisation keeps work files outside the personal side the malware runs in |
| Premium-rate / SMS (toll) fraud (Financial) | Joker / Bread | SEND_SMS is a runtime (dangerous) permission; premium short-code confirmation prompt (Android 4.2+); notification-listener access needs a separate user grant, limiting OTP theft | Play SMS/Call Log permission policy (2019) restricting SEND_SMS to default SMS apps; static + dynamic vetting of submissions; about 1,700 Bread apps caught before any download; post-publication removal | App allow-listing in the work profile; MTD flagging premium-SMS behaviour; FINDING: carrier-billing blocks on corporate SIMs are an operator control, outside MDM reach |
| Mobile botnet (Infrastructure abuse) | WireX | Background-execution limits and visible foreground-service notifications (Android 8+); per-app data-usage visibility. FINDING: INTERNET is a normal install-time permission, so the OS cannot stop bot traffic | Pre-publication vetting; post-publication removal of ~300 apps and Play Protect removal from already-infected devices | App allow-listing; per-app VPN / network monitoring for work apps; MTD detecting abnormal traffic; non-compliant devices blocked from corporate resources |
